# The White Cut

Campaign site for The White Cut — a barber in Chiang Mai (Hai Ya, Si Don Chai Soi 2).

`index.html` is the whole site: one file, no build step. Open it in a browser.

- English / ไทย toggle
- Services and prices, Old City locator map
- Booking: LINE Official Account button plus an on-page request form
- Staff back office at `#shop` (demo PIN `1122`): appointment book and cash ledger, saved on the device

A concept demo. The prices, the surreal copy and the LINE handle are placeholders; the contact
details are the shop's own (Instagram `@thewhitecut_cnx`, phone 099 008 5361).
